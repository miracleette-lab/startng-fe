## Chosen direction

A tiny SVG desk lamp sits in the homepage hero; its pupils gently track a mouse or pen pointer. It gives a playful game community a small sign of attention without interrupting play or adding a new dependency. The drawing is original and uses the existing theme colors. It is static on touch devices and when reduced motion is requested. Flowers, particle effects, 3D, sound, and any animation that gates or obscures content were deliberately left out.
